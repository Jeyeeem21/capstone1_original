║                           KEY IMPROVEMENTS                                   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  BEFORE:
  ❌ Failed deliveries had no automatic status change
  ❌ Users had to manually refresh to see updates
  ❌ Confusion about order status across portals
  ❌ Delayed response to delivery failures

  AFTER:
  ✅ Failed deliveries automatically move to "Return Requested"
  ✅ All portals update automatically every 5 seconds
  ✅ Real-time visibility across all users
  ✅ Immediate notifications for delivery failures
  ✅ Better workflow and user experience
